# Chinese VOC report contract

The analysis is organized as:

```text
社区 → 话题 → 用户/车辆与场景 → 要完成的任务 → 痛点及后果
     → 当前解决方案 → 方案不足 → 产品决策假设 → 下一步验证 → 原帖/评论证据
```

Every claim has `claim_status`:

- `fact`: directly stated by a post or comment;
- `inference`: a cross-evidence interpretation, explicitly labelled as such;
- `unknown`: the collected evidence cannot establish it.

Every formal topic should show, in Chinese:

1. 研究范围和实际覆盖日期；
2. 帖子、发帖作者、评论者和评论数量；
3. 平台、车型/年份/发动机和真实使用条件；
4. JTBD 用户任务；
5. Pain points with frequency, severity, and consequence where evidence supports them;
6. User needs/desired result;
7. Current products, brands, DIY or service workarounds;
8. Solution gaps and opposing evidence;
9. One of the six product decision types, labelled “机会假设”；
10. Evidence browser with original English, concise Chinese translation, and direct URL;
11. Coverage limits, failures, and unresolved validation questions.

Do not fill missing data with generic diesel-truck text. Do not show price, margin, manufacturing difficulty, market share, or user identity unless the source evidence or business data explicitly supports it.
